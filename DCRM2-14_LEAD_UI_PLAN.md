# DCRM2-14 Lead UI Plan

## Purpose

The Phase 2 Lead UI captures testing lead information and places valid leads into a queue for Phase 1 retrieval.

## Confirmed Lead Fields

- Name — required
- Email — required
- Phone — optional
- Campaign — required
- Source platform — required
- Consent status — required
- Queue status — generated by the system

## Source Platforms

The supported source platforms are:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- LinkedIn

## Queue Behaviour

When a valid testing lead is submitted, the system automatically assigns:

- Queue status: Pending
- Data status: Testing

Phase 2 stops after the lead has been inserted into the queue.

Phase 1 is responsible for later CRM processing.

## Out of Scope

The Phase 2 Lead UI does not perform:

- Lead scoring
- Lead qualification
- Lead conversion
- Sales pipeline stages
- CRM follow-up processing

These activities are outside the Phase 2 lead queue responsibility.
