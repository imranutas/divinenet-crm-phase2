Draft Sprint 2 Lead UI

The proposed Lead List may display:

- Lead ID
- Lead name
- Related campaign
- Facebook or Instagram source
- Consent status
- Lead status

The proposed Create Lead form may contain:

- Related campaign
- First name
- Last name
- Email
- Phone
- Source platform
- Consent status
- Lead status
- Save Draft button
- Cancel button
